# Introduction to the Agentic AI Era: Instructor Guide

**Module Time:** 120 minutes

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## What This Module Is About

This module introduces the architectural shift from **Generative AI
systems** to **Agentic AI systems**.

Learners often begin with a model-centric mindset: - LLMs generate text\
- Prompts produce responses\
- Accuracy equals success

This session reframes that thinking.

> Agentic AI is not about better responses.\
> It is about systems that execute goals.

The purpose of this module is to establish the architectural foundation
that the entire course builds upon.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Core Objectives

By the end of this module, learners should be able to:

-   Clearly distinguish Generative AI from Agentic AI\
-   Identify the architectural components that define an agent\
-   Differentiate between deterministic automation and adaptive agents\
-   Recognize why most enterprise AI failures are architectural\
-   Explain how autonomy, tools, and memory enable outcome creation

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Must-Hit Topics

-   Generative AI produces text; Agentic AI produces outcomes\
-   Agents require memory and tool execution\
-   Autonomy emerges from decision loops\
-   Deterministic automation is not agency\
-   Production reliability depends on architecture, not model size\
-   Real-world deployment requires integration, validation, and
    persistence

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. From Generative AI to Agentic Systems

**Time:** \~15 minutes

## Purpose

Shift learners from a linear response mindset to a loop-based execution
mindset.

## Key Talking Points

-   Traditional LLM interaction is stateless and reactive\
-   Agentic systems introduce persistence and execution\
-   The Observe → Think → Plan → Act → Store → Repeat loop\
-   Architectural differences determine business capability

## Critical Insight to Emphasize

The difference between Generative AI and Agentic AI is structural ---
not incremental.

Response vs Outcome is the defining shift.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Agents vs. Automated Tools

**Time:** \~20 minutes

## Purpose

Prevent conceptual confusion between automation and agency.

## Core Teaching Principle

Deterministic systems follow scripts.\
Agents reason within constraints and adapt dynamically.

## Critical Insight

LLM + tool does not automatically equal agent.

True agency requires: - Goal persistence\
- Iterative planning\
- Environmental feedback\
- State continuity

Autonomy exists on a spectrum --- and must be engineered deliberately.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Real-World Applications Across Industries

**Time:** \~10 minutes

## Purpose

Ground architectural theory in business reality.

## Key Talking Points

For each industry example, reinforce:

-   What problem is being solved\
-   Which architectural components enable it\
-   What constraint defines its boundary\
-   What autonomy level is appropriate

## Critical Insight to Emphasize

Model performance does not determine production success.\
System design does.

The chatbot failure case demonstrates that missing tools, APIs, and
memory cause breakdown --- not weak language generation.
